3 "Pro-Level" Tips to Feature in the GuideDirect-to-Cup Post-Brew Tuning: Remind users they don't even have to brew a full kettle to experiment! If a finished cup of coffee tastes flat or overly acidic, adding 1 drop of Buffer directly into the coffee cup instantly neutralizes sharp acidity, while 1 drop of Calcium heightens fruit sweetness on the spot.  Batch Water Pre-Mixing: Users can pre-add their drops into a 1-Gallon (3.8 L) or 5-Liter jug of distilled water (multiplying drop output by 7.6 or 10) so their everyday kettle refills are already mineralized.  No Scale Mornings: Emphasize the major friction win—brewers only use the 0.1 g scale once when mixing the 100 mL stock bottles. Every morning after that is 100% scale-free dropper dosing.

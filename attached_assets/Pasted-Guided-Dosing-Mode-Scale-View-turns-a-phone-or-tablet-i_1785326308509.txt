Guided Dosing Mode ("Scale View") turns a phone or tablet into a dedicated, hands-free lab monitor. When working with fine powders ($0.001\text{g}$ precision) or glass pipettes, looking down at tiny UI buttons on a mobile browser is a recipe for mistakes.Here is a full breakdown of how Scale View should look, function, and dynamically handle dosing mistakes on the fly.1. Visual SpecificationsHigh-Contrast Dark Mode Base: True OLED black (#000000) background with ultra-bright neon readout text (emerald green for target, amber for warnings) to eliminate screen glare under bright kitchen or bar lights.Massive Typography: The current target weight and active mineral name dominate $60\%$ of the screen width.Proximity Gauge (Target Bar): A horizontal visual slider with a central "Green Zone" ($\pm 1\text{--}2\%$ tolerance).As you approach the target, the bar fills from blue $\rightarrow$ green.If you overshoot, the bar instantly flashes amber or red past the center marker.Dual Weight Tracker (Solves "Did I Tare?" Anxiety):Current Step Target: Grams/drops needed for this specific mineral.Cumulative Container Weight: The expected total mass added so far ($m_{\text{total}} = \sum m_i$). If a user forgets whether they tared their scale between ingredients, they can verify against the total vessel weight.Prevent Screen Timeout: Uses the Web Screen Wake Lock API (navigator.wakeLock) so the screen never dim or sleeps mid-dose.2. Dynamic Overshoot & Under-Dose Correction EngineOvershooting a dry salt by $0.040\text{g}$ or dumping two extra drops of liquid concentrate happens constantly. Rather than forcing the user to scrap the batch and start over, Scale View features a Real-Time Re-balancing Engine.When an overshoot or under-dose occurs outside the acceptable tolerance band, the user taps "Adjust / Re-balance" (or the app prompts automatically), presenting two intelligent correction strategies:                      [ OVERSHOOT DETECTED ]

Strategy A: Total Volume Auto-Scaling (Simplest & Exact)If an ingredient is overshot, scale the target water volume proportionally:$$V_{\text{new}} = V_{\text{target}} \times \frac{m_{\text{actual}}}{m_{\text{target}}}$$Example: User targeted $0.420\text{g}$ Epsom salt for $5.0\text{L}$ of water, but accidentally poured $0.485\text{g}$.App Action: The app recalculates: "Target volume updated to 5.77L of RO water. Remaining mineral targets have been recalculated to match."Strategy B: Cross-Mineral Ratio Tuning (Fixed Volume Workflows)If the user cannot change their water container size (e.g., they are filling a fixed $5\text{L}$ jug), scaling total volume isn't an option. The engine offers a mineral rebalance:Calculate New Baseline Ion: If $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ is overshot, calculate the new resulting General Hardness ($\text{GH}$) contribution.Adjust Complementary Ions:Option to keep original $\text{Ca:Mg}$ ratio by increasing Calcium Chloride ($\text{CaCl}_2$) to match.Option to accept the higher $\text{GH}$ and adjust Carbonate Hardness ($\text{KH}$ / Buffer) proportionally to preserve the intended flavor balance ratio ($\text{GH:KH}$).Strategy C: Green-Zone Tolerance Thresholds (Ignore Micro-Variances)Not every milligram matters. The app calculates the actual PPM shift of the error:If an error shifts overall hardness by $< 1.5\text{ ppm as CaCO}_3$, the app shows a green badge:✓ Minor Overshoot (+1.2 ppm GH). Flavor impact negligible — Safe to proceed.3.

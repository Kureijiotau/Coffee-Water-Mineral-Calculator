---
name: Standalone concentrate workspace
description: Concentrate creation is an independent one-stock-at-a-time app workspace.
---

Concentrate creation lives in its own app-header tab, separate from Brewer, Alchemist, and Watermancer. The workflow is standalone and weight-based: choose one salt and hydration form, set any strength from 1% upward and a total stock weight, then weigh, calibrate, and preview final-water dosing.

**Why:** The user wants concentrate tools consolidated without adding more clutter to the recipe workflows, their physical setup uses separate small dropper bottles, and tiny liquid volumes are difficult to measure without syringes.

**How to apply:** Keep this tab focused on one stock at a time. Treat concentration as % w/w: compute salt grams from total stock grams and water grams as the remainder. Calibrate the selected stock by weighing a known number of drops; report grams/drop, salt mg/drop, and final-water contribution. Reuse shared salt definitions, hydration forms, and solubility warnings, but keep stock-specific weight calibration local so it does not overwrite the calculator’s volume-based calibration.

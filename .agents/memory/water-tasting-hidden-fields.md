---
name: Water tasting hidden fields
description: Preserve historical tasting data while simplifying the active Water Tasting form
---

**Rule:** Keep new Water Tasting notes focused on the five affective cup-impact ratings, water profile, and optional coffee name. When editing an older CVA note, carry forward its hidden descriptive scores, descriptors, and coffee metadata without converting its scoring format. Keep legacy-format notes in their original storage schema.

**Why:** The active form should focus on how water changes the cup, while saved notes remain intact and editable.

**How to apply:** When removing or hiding fields from Water Tasting, preserve their saved values during edit/save and avoid data migrations unless the user explicitly requests one.

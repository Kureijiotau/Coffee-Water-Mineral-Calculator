---
name: Alchemist and Watermancer language
description: Alchemist labels should use Watermancer vocabulary for waters, hydration forms, salt choice, and dose
---

Use Watermancer terminology in Alchemist wherever the concepts overlap: Add waters, Preparation route, Salt contribution, Choose salts, hydration form, Dose, Prefer sulfate, Prefer chloride, and Don’t care.

**Why:** Shared language makes the two workflows easier to compare without changing the Alchemist’s recipe and concentrate behavior.

**How to apply:** Align visible labels, helper copy, and accessibility labels while leaving calculations and control behavior unchanged.

The user requires every concentrate calculation and stock size to be mass-based; never use actual volume in concentrate math. Explain that plain water's mL and grams are approximately interchangeable, but keep that as a convenience note only. The requested preparation order is: put the bottle on the scale, tare, add the salt, add water by mass to the target, leave shake headspace, and shake to dissolve.

**Why:** The user finds volume measurements confusing and said concentrate calculations must always be mass-based. They explicitly requested a salt-first, water-by-mass preparation sequence with room to shake.

**How to apply:** Audit calculations, preparation instructions, dosing, and saved-stock handling together. Show mL/g interchangeability only for water; never treat that as a volume-based concentrate formula. Keep old volume-based stocks explicitly identified rather than silently reinterpreting them as mass-based.

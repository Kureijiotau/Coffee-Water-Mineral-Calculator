---
name: Mode identity language
description: The three workspaces need distinct user-facing roles
---

Use this positioning: Brewer is the simple flavor-first recipe path; Alchemist is the mineral recipe lab for designing salts, hydration forms, dose balance, and repeatable batches or concentrates; Watermancer is the ion-target and source-water matching workspace.

**Why:** Alchemist previously sounded like a more complex Brewer, making the three modes feel indistinct.

**How to apply:** Keep Alchemist copy centered on designing the recipe behind the cup and preparing it reliably, while reserving final ion targets and source-water optimization for Watermancer.

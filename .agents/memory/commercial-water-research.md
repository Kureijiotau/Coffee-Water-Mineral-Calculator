---
name: Commercial water research boundary
description: Commercial mineral-water research is stored as structured candidates and only independently verified entries are promoted to the live catalog.
---

The live Watermancer catalog may include commercial waters, but Gemini research candidates must remain reviewable until their core ion profile and source are independently verified. Catalog entries should use the existing ion keys and avoid duplicate names or matching compositions.

**Why:** Bottled-water analyses vary by country, product format, and label date, and a plausible-looking research response is not sufficient provenance for chemistry data users may dose from.

**How to apply:** Keep broad research in a structured candidate file; insert only verified candidates into the database and synchronized API fallback data. Preserve still/carbonated status and source metadata in the research record even if the current catalog schema cannot store all of it.

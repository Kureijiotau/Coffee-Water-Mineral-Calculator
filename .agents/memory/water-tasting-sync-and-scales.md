---
name: Water Tasting sync and scales
description: User-approved boundaries for cross-device saved tastings, sensory intensity wording, and notes.
---

Only saved Water Tastings sync across signed-in devices; active drafts stay on the device. Scope signed-in local records by account, merge guest records at first sign-in, and merge records by ID using the newest change time. Keep timestamped deletion markers so an older offline copy cannot restore a deleted tasting; a later edit may supersede a deletion.

Keep legacy scoring records and hidden legacy values intact. New sensory sliders describe intensity rather than judging quality, with distinct attribute-specific cues and color ramps. Mouthfeel wording should describe tactile weight and texture. Optional notes are limited to 2,000 characters. Do not use “battery acid” literally.

**Why:** These constraints preserve existing tasting records while supporting the approved multi-device workflow and neutral, attribute-specific sensory ratings.

**How to apply:** Use these rules whenever changing Water Tasting persistence, account sync, scoring labels, slider presentation, or note editing.

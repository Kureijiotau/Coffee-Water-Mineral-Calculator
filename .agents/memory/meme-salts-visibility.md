name: Meme salts visibility
description: Calcium Lactate and Magnesium Glycinate are intentionally hidden from the default salt table and revealed through the meme control.
---

Keep Calcium Lactate and Magnesium Glycinate in the shared chemistry catalog, but do not show their rows in the default salts table. The small meme control is the deliberate reveal path.

**Why:** These salts remain valid for saved recipes and chemistry calculations, while the main table should stay focused on the standard salt set.

**How to apply:** Preserve their catalog IDs and calculation behavior. Change visibility through the salts-card reveal control rather than removing the salts from the underlying model.

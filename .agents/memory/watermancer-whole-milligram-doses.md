---
name: Watermancer whole-milligram doses
description: Physical salt amounts shown and applied after matching
---

Watermancer converts matched salt doses to the nearest practical whole milligram before applying the route, then recomputes final ions from those quantized doses. Zero remains zero and any positive dose stays at least 1 mg.

**Why:** Users weigh the displayed recipe, so leaving solver precision in the instructions makes the reported ion profile differ from the dose they can actually add.

**How to apply:** Quantize at route application and keep the active dose table, best-match preview, preparation steps, and final-ion calculations on that same quantized route. Manual dose edits remain user-controlled.

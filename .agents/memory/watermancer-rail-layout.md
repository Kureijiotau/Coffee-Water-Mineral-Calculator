---
name: Watermancer rail layout
description: Workflow navigation is vertical on wide screens and horizontal on smaller screens
---

The Watermancer workflow rail has six destinations: Set target, Add waters, Add salts, Choose route, Closest match, and Final mixture. It stays horizontal below wide-screen breakpoints and becomes a compact sticky vertical rail on the right at wide desktop sizes.

**Why:** The long Watermancer workspace benefits from persistent desktop navigation while avoiding a narrow or intrusive side rail on smaller screens.

**How to apply:** Keep the closest-match and final-mixture cards as separate scroll destinations, and preserve the responsive horizontal-to-vertical transition when adjusting the workflow rail.

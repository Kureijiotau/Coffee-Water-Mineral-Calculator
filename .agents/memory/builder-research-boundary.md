---
name: Builder research boundary
description: Research-inspired builder UX may evolve independently while established chemistry calculations remain the source of truth.
---

Builder improvements can use research-backed flavor guidance, profile visualizations, and preparation-safety education, but new research formulas must not replace the established salt, ion, hydration, water-mixing, or dosing engine without an explicit product decision.

**Why:** The calculator’s shared chemistry paths already encode important source-water, co-ion, hydration, bicarbonate, and correction behavior that builder-facing experiments should not silently change.

**How to apply:** Prefer derived display-only guidance and builder-panel interaction improvements. Keep Brewer focused on flavor shaping and the live dosing cockpit; keep the full Mineral Salts editor and recipe-library controls in Alchemist/Watermancer. Route any actual recipe change through the existing suggestion and shared calculation paths.

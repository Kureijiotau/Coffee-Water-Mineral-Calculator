---
name: Conservative concentrate strength
description: Product rule for recommending and overriding precipitation-aware concentrate strengths.
---

All-in-one and separated-stock recommendations must account for the active salts in each bottle, their amounts, selected forms, and bottle volume. Use the strongest warning-free modeled strength for that bottle, then leave a modest safety margin below it. AIO evaluates every active salt together; GH + KH evaluates each bottle independently.

**Why:** A strength can remain below individual solubility limits yet still precipitate when salts share one bottle, while separated stocks can still have interactions within a bottle. Users should not spend time preparing a stock that is likely to chalk up.

**How to apply:** Present the conservative value as the recommended ceiling for AIO and for each GH/KH bottle. Allow users to enter a stronger value, but show a prominent precipitation-risk warning; for AIO, recommend separate GH and KH concentrates. Keep each stock's calculation independent.

For PeakKitchen's reconstructed recipes, the user says precipitation is part of how the formulations are built, not a defect to warn about or fix unless they ask.

**Why:** The user clarified that precipitation observed in these recipes is expected by design.

**How to apply:** Do not add generic precipitation warnings to PeakKitchen recipe cards or alter their formulations solely to prevent this behavior. Keep this exception specific to PeakKitchen.

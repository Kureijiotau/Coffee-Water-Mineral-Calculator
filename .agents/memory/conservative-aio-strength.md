---
name: Conservative all-in-one strength
description: Product rule for recommending and overriding all-in-one concentrate strengths.
---

All-in-one concentrate recommendations must account for the active salts, their amounts, selected forms, and bottle volume. Use the strongest warning-free modeled strength, then leave a modest safety margin below it. Do not reuse the looser compatible-stock ceiling.

**Why:** A strength can remain below individual solubility limits yet still precipitate when every salt shares one bottle. Users should not spend time preparing an AIO stock that is likely to chalk up.

**How to apply:** Present the conservative value as the recommended AIO maximum. Allow users to enter a stronger value, but show a prominent precipitation-risk warning and recommend separate GH and KH concentrates. Keep split-stock ceilings independent.

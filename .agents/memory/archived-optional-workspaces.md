name: Archived optional workspaces
description: Product boundary for retired Brewer, assistant, scanner, and guide/profile features
---

Retired optional features should remain preserved as dormant source for possible reintegration, while the live calculator keeps Alchemist as its default and exposes only the intentional core workspaces.

**Why:** The product needs a smaller live surface and lower startup/render cost without losing earlier experiments or research-backed flows that may be useful later.

**How to apply:** Keep the seven-day Robert Asami guide as a separate lazy-loaded entry point. Do not register or import the Water Intent Assistant, Brewer mode, Taste Profile, coffee-preference modal, Workframe builder, or Label Scanner into the live flow unless they are deliberately reactivated. Never restore Brewer automatically when loading older saved plans.

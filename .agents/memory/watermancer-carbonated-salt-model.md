---
name: SodaStream bicarbonate salt model
description: The Watermancer magnesium hydroxide and calcium carbonate entries represent the fully carbonated bicarbonate products in final water.
---

For the SodaStream-specific Watermancer salts, the entered dose is the dry starting powder mass. Model complete conversion in final water:

- Mg(OH)₂ + 2CO₂ → Mg²⁺ + 2HCO₃⁻
- CaCO₃ + CO₂ + H₂O → Ca²⁺ + 2HCO₃⁻

The resulting bicarbonate mass includes material incorporated during carbonation; it is not limited to the original powder mass. The selected model assumes the powder fully dissolves and the bicarbonate remains dissolved through use. The separate 1 mg/drop guide has not yet established a preparation method for these two entries.

**Why:** The user asked for the resulting magnesium- and calcium-bicarbonate water, not just the cation contribution of the starting powder.

**How to apply:** Keep Watermancer targets and reported readings in final-water ion terms. Do not model these entries as ordinary dry bicarbonate powders, and do not claim their stock is 1 mg of powder per drop until the guide method is confirmed.

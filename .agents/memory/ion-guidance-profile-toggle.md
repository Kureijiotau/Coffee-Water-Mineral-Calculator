---
name: Ion guidance profile toggle
description: Relationship between Aiki and Watermancer Sensory flag profiles
---

Keep Aiki’s original ion guidance and Watermancer Sensory guidance as separate selectable profiles.

**Why:** Aiki’s ranges are an established light-roast pourover baseline, while the newer ranges are broader research-informed sensory suggestions; replacing either would erase useful context.

**How to apply:** The selected profile may drive green/yellow/red guidance and comparison bars, but must not alter chemistry calculations, solver targets, ceilings, or dosing behavior.

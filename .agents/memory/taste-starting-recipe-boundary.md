---
name: Taste starting recipe boundary
description: The coffee-preference questionnaire's intended scope and promise
---

The coffee-preference questionnaire is a Brewer-only onboarding shortcut that generates a tunable mineral recipe starting point; it does not identify bottled water or claim roaster-specific chemistry.

**Why:** The original global “Find My Water” label was ambiguous, and the roaster answer did not affect the calculation.

**How to apply:** Keep the action near Brewer flavor controls, describe the result as a starting recipe, and warn that applying it replaces the current Brewer mineral recipe.

---
name: Recipe-card recovery QR
description: Product boundary for presenting recovery and share QR codes on exported recipe cards.
---

Keep the embedded recovery QR visually quiet and do not label it as a user-facing recovery action. Only the share-link QR should receive a visible action label.

**Why:** The recovery QR primarily supports app-side recipe import. Presenting it as a prominent user option makes exported cards feel technical and visually cluttered.

**How to apply:** Preserve scan contrast and quiet-zone requirements, but integrate the recovery code as subdued card metadata. Use explicit labels only for actions the recipient is expected to take, such as opening a share link.

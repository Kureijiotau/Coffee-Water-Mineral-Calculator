name: Coffee-water amounts and balance
description: Quantitative research boundary for ion unpleasantness thresholds, ratios, and total mineral balance
---

Treat ion taste thresholds and “ideal” ratios as context-dependent guidance, not universal chemistry rules.

**Why:** Brew method, coffee, pH, alkalinity, hardness, total mineral load, and sensory context interact; published brand recipes are practical conventions rather than universal optima, and coffee-specific unpleasantness data is sparse.

**How to apply:** Keep ranges labeled by evidence type and matrix (water versus beverage). Separate experimentally supported relationships from industry heuristics, especially Cl:SO4 and simple total-ppm targets. Do not change solver ceilings or targets from these findings without separate validation.

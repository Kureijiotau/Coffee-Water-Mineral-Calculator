3
Combine the salt concentrate
Reserve 500 mL of the prepared water for the salt concentrate. Dissolve the salts completely, then add the concentrate to the remaining water, rinse the vessel into the batch, and stir thoroughly.
4
Verify and brew
Check for approximately 1197 ppm TDS. The water should be clear and all minerals fully dissolved. Proceed with your brew method and adjust extraction to taste.

Small amounts are difficult to weigh accurately. For better consistency, multiply the recipe for a larger batch or use a concentrate.
